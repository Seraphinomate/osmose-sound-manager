Osmose Sound Manager is a free, browser-based preset manager for the Expressive E Osmose. Search 609 sounds, remember your macro settings, and route one expression pedal to as many destinations as you like — each with its own range.

What it does

Everything around the sound

OSM doesn't edit patches — the EaganMatrix stays where it is. It handles what the instrument makes hard: finding a sound among 609, keeping your macro positions, and playing a set without scrolling an encoder.

01

Search and filter

Full Factory and Expansion 01 library. Filter by bank, by 14 instrument types, and by 39 character tags at once — combinations that return nothing are hidden as you go.

02

Macro memory

Six macros per preset, remembered and re-sent when you come back. Name them, save named snapshots, set your own neutral starting point, and freeze positions across preset changes.

03

Spider pedal routing

Send one expression pedal to any of six macros plus FX Mix and Postgain, each with its own minimum and maximum. Reverse a range and two parameters trade against each other in a single sweep.

04

Playlists

Ordered set lists with colours and drag-to-reorder. Arrow keys and space bar drive them, so a page-turner pedal can run a whole set.

05

Full control surface

Pregain, Postgain, four FX params, FX Mix, EQ tilt and pivot, compressor mix, sustain and sostenuto — all on one page, with two external controllers supported.

06

One file

No install, no account, no server. Open the HTML file in Chrome or Edge and it works, online or off. Your data exports to a single JSON you own.

Get it

Download

One file. Save it anywhere and open it in Chrome or Edge. Connect the Osmose over USB, accept the MIDI permission, and choose port 2 in Settings — the manual walks through it in a minute.

Who made this, and what to expect

Jonathan Pulichino

Osmose owner, musician, and occasional builder of tools I wish existed. This one started as something I made for my own instrument and kept growing. I hope it makes your sessions smoother.

It's a side project built on evenings and weekends — not a product, and not a company. That shapes what follows.
