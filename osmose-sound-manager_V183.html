MIDI Connection

Output: Osmose Port 2 (macOS) / MIDIOUT2 Osmose (Win)
Controller 1: LPD8 or any knob/slider controller — CC 12–17 → 6 macros
Controller 2: Nord Piano 5 or any device with expression pedal — source CC configurable in the Pedal Macro zone

Controller → Osmose routing
When enabled: CCs received from the controller are forwarded to Osmose (ch 1) and reflected on the macro knobs in real-time. Assign your controller's knobs or sliders to CC 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 to control the 6 macros.

CC Filter — Controllers → Osmose

Disabled CCs are blocked everywhere — not forwarded to Osmose and not usable as Pedal Macro source. Useful to prevent sustain pedal or other controllers from interfering with the Osmose.
